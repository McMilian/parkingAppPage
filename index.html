How it works

Up and running in three steps.

Parking Spot Share works community by community. Only residents of your building can join, so you're always sharing with your neighbours.

1

Get your join code

Every community has its own code. Ask your community admin for it — without it, nobody can join.

2

Create your account

Enter the code, then sign up securely with Microsoft. Parking Spot Share never sees your password.

3

Add your apartment and spot

Add your apartment number and, if you have one, your parking space — up to three. That's it.

Share your spot

Away for the day? Let your spot work for you.

Choose the hours you won't need it, add a note if you like, and post. Your neighbours see the offer straight away.

  • A few hours or a whole week — you choose
  • Notes like “covered, near the lift”
  • Edit or cancel any time before it's booked

Nothing on offer? Ask, and your neighbours get a ping.

Post a request for the time you need. Everyone in your community with a parking space gets a push notification. When one of them accepts, the booking is created for you automatically.

And there's more

Small touches that add up.

Your community at a glance

The home screen shows how many offers and requests are active right now, and how many neighbours have joined.

Notifications that matter

Get notified when a neighbour needs a spot, when your request is accepted and when a spot is freed up early.

English and Polish

Use the app in your language and switch any time in Settings. Notifications follow your choice.

Up to three spaces

Own or rent more than one spot? Register up to three and share each one on its own schedule.

Flexible time ranges

Find offers for the next 24 hours, 3 days or a week, or pick exact dates and times.

Always editable

Change an offer's times or note until someone books it, and update your parking spaces whenever you need.

Privacy

Built for neighbours.
Private by design.

Parking Spot Share shows other residents only what they need to share a parking spot with you — and nothing more.

What Residents of your community A neighbour who shared their spot with you Anyone else
Your nameon your offers, requests and bookings
Apartment numberso they can reach you about the car on their spot
Email and passwordyour password is handled by Microsoft only

Invite-only

Each community is protected by a join code. Offers and requests are visible only to its residents.

No passwords stored

Sign-in is handled by Microsoft Entra. Parking Spot Share only receives a token confirming who you are.

No ads, no tracking

No advertising, no third-party analytics, no location tracking. Your data is never sold.

Delete any time

Remove your account and all your data yourself, straight from Settings.
